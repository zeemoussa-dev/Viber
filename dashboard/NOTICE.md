# Vendored UI

`src/styles/*.css`, `src/ui/*` (except `InfoButton.tsx` and `PageHeader.tsx`, which are Viber's own
simplified versions) and `public/fonts/*` are copied from the `second_brain` repository's frontend
(`src/frontend/src/styles`, `src/frontend/src/components`, `src/frontend/public/fonts`), the same owner's
private project, so the two apps share one look. They are Viber's own copies: edit them freely, and do not
expect them to track upstream. Fonts are SIL Open Font License; their licence texts are in
`public/fonts/licences/`.
